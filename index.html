Hey, I'm Kat de Jesus Chua.

I'm a data scientist, writer, and civic data transparency activist based in Chicago.

I currently cover local government meetings as part of City Bureau's Documenters network. You can find notes I've written here.

I was a 2025 Investigative Reporters and Editors Gary Marx Fellow, recognized for my work building Python pipelines to preserve public data for the Data Liberation Project.

Previously, I worked as a Data Scientist at Warner Bros. Discovery, specializing in NLP and applied ML for media content. I graduated in 2020 with a B.A. in Data Science from the University of California, Berkeley. Find my resume here.

Chicago Documenters

Covering Chicago Government Meetings

As a Documenter, I attend and take notes on Chicago's public meetings.

These notes support City Bureau's reporting and provide Chicagoans a record of what happens in local government meetings, neighborhood councils, and courthouses in their city.

Data Liberation Project

Preserving Public Datasets

Vast quantities of government data exist in inaccessible, difficult-to-analyze formats. This data is also vulnerable to removal or manipulation by bad actors.

As a volunteer, I build webscraping and data cleansing pipelines to preserve this data for the public good.
