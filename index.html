Chicago Documenters

As a Documenter, I attend and take notes on Chicago's public meetings.

These notes help inform City Bureau's reporting, as well as fellow citizens on what happens in their city's neighborhood councils, government buildings, and courthouses.

Data Liberation Project

Preserving Public Datasets

Vast quantities of government data exist in inaccessible, difficult-to-analyze formats. This data is also vulnerable to removal or manipulation by bad actors.

As a volunteer, I build webscraping and data cleansing pipelines to preserve this data for the public good.

MuckRock

TSA Complaint Data Disappeared

At the start of Trump's second term, the TSA removed years of complaints reports from its website.

I covered the removal and how Data Liberation Project's archive of TSA complaints data can fill the gap.
